OnePDF Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

OnePDF is designed to scan documents on your device and create a PDF for you to share.

Data Collection

OnePDF does not collect personal data.

OnePDF does not require an account, does not use analytics, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Camera Access

OnePDF asks for camera access so you can scan documents with the built-in iOS document scanner. Scanned pages are processed on your device.

Documents and PDFs

Scanned pages and generated PDFs stay on your device while you use the app. When you tap Share PDF, OnePDF uses the iOS share sheet so you can choose where to send or save the PDF. The destination you choose may have its own privacy practices.

Third-Party Services

OnePDF does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, cloud document storage, or account services.
