Common Questions

Does OnePDF upload my scans?

No. OnePDF creates PDFs on your device and uses the iOS share sheet when you choose to share or save the PDF.

Why does OnePDF need camera access?

OnePDF uses the camera through the built-in iOS document scanner to capture document pages.

How do I clear a scan?

After sharing, OnePDF clears the current scan. You can also delete pages before sharing.
